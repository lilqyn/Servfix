## FOR BUYERS

### Article 1: How to Create Your SERVFIX Account

**Summary:** Get started on SERVFIX in under 2 minutes. Here's how to create your account and set up your profile.

**Body:**

Getting started on SERVFIX is quick and easy.

**Step 1: Download the app**
Search for "SERVFIX" on Google Play and install it. You can also use the web app at www.servfixgh.com.

**Step 2: Sign up**
Tap "Sign Up" and enter your name, email, and password. You can also sign up with your Google account for faster registration.

**Step 3: Choose your role**
Select "Buyer" if you're looking to hire service providers. You can always contact support later if you want to switch to a provider account.

**Step 4: Complete your profile**
Add a profile photo and your location. This helps providers in your area find and serve you better.

That's it. You're ready to browse and book services.

### Article 2: How to Search and Find Providers

**Summary:** Learn how to browse categories, search by keyword, and filter results to find the perfect provider for your needs.

**Body:**

SERVFIX makes it easy to find the right service provider.

### Article 3: How to Book a Service

**Summary:** A step-by-step guide to booking your first service on SERVFIX, from browsing to checkout.

**Body:**

Booking on SERVFIX takes less than 2 minutes.

**Step 1: Find a provider**
Browse categories or search for the service you need. Tap on a provider to see their full profile.

**Step 2: Choose a service tier**
Most providers offer different tiers (Basic, Standard, Premium) with different scopes and prices. Select the one that matches your needs.

**Step 3: Add to cart and checkout**
Tap "Add to Cart" and proceed to checkout. You can add notes about your specific requirements, preferred dates, or location details.

**Step 4: Pay securely**
Choose your payment method — Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo), debit/credit card, or bank transfer. Your payment is protected until the job is done.

**Step 5: Wait for acceptance**
The provider will be notified and can accept your order. You'll receive a notification once they accept.

**Step 6: Approve and review**
After the provider completes the work and submits delivery, review the results. If you're satisfied, approve to release payment. Then leave a review to help other buyers.

### Article 4: How Payments and Protection Work

**Summary:** Understand how SERVFIX keeps your money safe with secure payment protection, and when funds are released to providers.

**Body:**

Your money is always protected on SERVFIX.

**What is Payment Protection?**
Payment Protection means your payment is held by a secure, licensed payment partner — not given directly to the provider. Think of it as a safe that holds your money until the job is done.

**How the flow works:**
1. You pay at checkout via Mobile Money, card, or bank transfer
2. Your money is held securely until the job is done
3. The provider completes the work
4. You review the delivery and approve
5. Payment is released to the provider

**What if I'm not satisfied?**
If the work isn't up to standard, don't approve. Instead, tap "Report Issue" to open a dispute. Our support team will review both sides and work toward a fair resolution — including a full or partial refund if warranted.

**Auto-release:**
If you don't respond within the review window (24-72 hours depending on order amount), funds are automatically released to the provider. So make sure to review deliveries promptly.

**Refunds:**
If you cancel before the service starts, you receive a full refund. Refunds are processed back to your original payment method.

### Article 6: How to Open a Dispute

**Summary:** Something went wrong with your order? Here's how to open a dispute and get it resolved fairly.

**Body:**

SERVFIX has built-in dispute resolution to protect you.

**When to open a dispute:**
Open a dispute if the provider delivered work that doesn't match what was agreed, if the quality is unacceptable, or if the provider didn't complete the service.

**How to open one:**
1. Go to your Orders page
2. Select the order in question
3. Tap "Report Issue"
4. Choose a reason and describe the problem
5. Upload photos or screenshots as evidence

**What happens next:**
Your payment is frozen while our support team investigates. We review evidence from both sides — your report and the provider's response.

**Possible outcomes:**
- Full refund to you
- Partial refund (you keep part, provider keeps part)
- Release to provider (if the dispute is not upheld)

**Timing:**
Most disputes are resolved within 3-5 business days. You'll receive notifications as the case progresses.

**Prevention is better:**
Chat with your provider before booking to align on scope, timeline, and expectations. Clear communication prevents most disputes.

### Article 7: How to Cancel an Order and Get a Refund

**Summary:** Need to cancel? Here's when and how you can cancel an order and what happens to your payment.

**Body:**

Sometimes plans change. Here's how cancellation works on SERVFIX.

**When can I cancel?**
You can cancel an order any time before the provider starts work. If the order status is "Created" or "Payment Secured" and the provider hasn't accepted yet, cancellation is straightforward.

**How to cancel:**
1. Go to Orders
2. Select the order
3. Tap "Cancel Order"
4. Confirm

**What about my money?**
If you already paid, a full refund is automatically initiated. The refund goes back to your original payment method (Mobile Money or card). Processing times depend on your payment provider, but most refunds arrive within 1-3 business days.

**After the provider starts work:**
Once the provider accepts and begins work, you can't cancel directly. If there's an issue, you should open a dispute after delivery is submitted.

### Article 11: How to Submit Delivery and Get Paid

**Summary:** You've completed the work. Here's how to submit delivery and receive your payment.

**Body:**

After completing the service, here's how to get paid.

**Step 1: Submit delivery**
Go to the order and tap "Submit Delivery." Add a message describing what was done, and upload photos of the completed work if applicable.

**Step 2: Buyer reviews**
The buyer has a review window to inspect the work:
- Orders under GHS 500: 24 hours
- GHS 500 - GHS 2,000: 48 hours
- Over GHS 2,000: 72 hours

**Step 3: Payment released**
When the buyer approves (or the review window expires with no dispute), your earnings are credited to your SERVFIX wallet. The 10% platform fee is deducted automatically.

**Step 4: Withdraw**
Go to your Wallet and tap "Request Payout." Select your Mobile Money network (MTN, Vodafone, or AirtelTigo), enter the amount, and confirm.

**Daily limits:**
- Unverified providers: GHS 1,000/day
- Verified providers: GHS 5,000/day
- Maximum single payout: GHS 10,000

Complete your KYC verification to access higher limits.

### Article 12: How to Price Your Services

**Summary:** Not sure what to charge? Here's a practical pricing guide for service providers in Ghana.

**Body:**

Good pricing wins more customers than low pricing.

**Calculate your base cost:**
Add up: Materials + Labour + Transport. This is your minimum — you should never charge less than this.

**Add your profit margin:**
A standard margin is 20-40% on top of your base cost. For example, if your base cost is GHS 100, charge GHS 120-140.

**Be transparent:**
State clearly what's included in each price tier. "Includes labour, basic materials, and cleanup" is much better than just a number. Customers hate surprises.

**Research the market:**
Check what other providers in your category charge on SERVFIX. You don't need to be the cheapest — but you need to justify your price with quality photos and reviews.

**Offer tiers:**
Create Basic, Standard, and Premium tiers. This gives customers options and often results in higher average orders. For example:
- Basic: Simple repair, 1 hour
- Standard: Full service, up to 3 hours, includes materials
- Premium: Complete overhaul, warranty included

**Review and adjust:**
Check your pricing every month. If you're getting lots of views but few bookings, your price might be too high. If every order is instant, you might be undercharging.

### Article 13: How to Handle Disputes as a Provider

**Summary:** A customer opened a dispute. Don't panic. Here's how to respond and protect your earnings.

**Body:**

Disputes happen. How you handle them determines the outcome.

**Stay calm and professional:**
When you receive a dispute notification, don't react emotionally. Read the buyer's complaint carefully and understand what they're unhappy about.

**Respond with evidence:**
Provide your side of the story with supporting evidence. Upload photos of the completed work, screenshots of chat conversations where scope was agreed, and any other documentation.

**Common dispute reasons and how to prevent them:**
- "Work not as described" — Always confirm scope in chat before starting
- "Provider didn't show up" — Communicate delays immediately
- "Poor quality" — Take before/after photos as proof of work done
- "Overcharged" — Agree on pricing in writing before starting

**Possible outcomes:**
- Full release to you (dispute denied)
- Partial refund (split between you and buyer)
- Full refund to buyer (dispute upheld)

**Prevention is key:**
The best way to handle disputes is to prevent them. Communicate clearly, set expectations upfront, document your work, and deliver on your promises.

### Article 16: Understanding Taxes and Compliance in Ghana

**Summary:** A quick guide to tax obligations for service providers earning through SERVFIX.

**Body:**

As a service provider earning income through SERVFIX, you should understand your basic tax obligations in Ghana.

**TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number):**
Every person earning income in Ghana needs a TIN. Register at your nearest GRA (Ghana Revenue Authority) office or online at gra.gov.gh. It's free.

**Income tax:**
Your earnings from SERVFIX are taxable income. Ghana uses a graduated tax rate for self-employed individuals. Keep records of all your earnings and expenses.

**VAT:**
If your annual turnover exceeds GHS 200,000, you're required to register for VAT (currently 15%). Below this threshold, you're not required to charge VAT.

**Record keeping:**
SERVFIX provides a full transaction history in your wallet. Download your earnings reports regularly for your tax records.

**What SERVFIX provides:**
- Complete order and payment history
- Earnings breakdown per period
- Platform fee deductions clearly documented

**Disclaimer:**
This is general guidance only. For specific tax advice, consult a qualified accountant or the Ghana Revenue Authority.

### Article 18: Getting Verified on SERVFIX

**Summary:** Verification unlocks higher payout limits and builds customer trust. Here's how to get verified.

**Body:**

Verification is required before you can receive payouts and gives your profile a verified badge that builds customer trust.

**What verification means:**
A verified provider has confirmed their identity through our KYC (Know Your Customer) process. The verified badge tells customers that you are who you say you are.

**Benefits of verification:**
- Required for all payouts
- Higher daily payout limits (GHS 5,000 vs GHS 1,000)
- Verified badge on your profile
- Higher trust and more bookings

**How to get verified:**
1. Go to Settings in the app
2. Tap "Verification"
3. Submit the required documents
4. Wait for review (usually 1-3 business days)

**What you'll need:**
- A valid Ghana Card, Passport, or Voter's ID
- A clear selfie for identity matching

**After verification:**
Once approved, your verified badge appears on your profile immediately. You can now request payouts to your Mobile Money account up to GHS 5,000 per day.

If your verification is rejected, you'll receive a message explaining why. You can resubmit with corrected documents.
